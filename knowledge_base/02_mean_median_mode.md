# Mean, Median and Mode

## Topic Area
Business Statistics - Descriptive Statistics

## Learning Level
Beginner to Intermediate

## Concept Explanation
Mean, median and mode are measures of central tendency. They help summarize a dataset using one representative value.

Mean shows the average value. Median shows the middle value. Mode shows the most frequently occurring value.

## Key Formula
Mean = Sum of all observations / Number of observations

## Formula Meaning
- Sum of all observations means adding all values in the dataset.
- Number of observations means the total count of values.

## Simple Example
Data: 10, 20, 30

Mean = (10 + 20 + 30) / 3 = 20

Data: 12, 15, 18

Median = 15 because it is the middle value.

Data: 10, 12, 12, 15

Mode = 12 because it appears most often.

## Business Use Case
A sales manager may use mean sales to understand average performance. A human resource manager may use median salary to understand the typical employee salary when a few very high salaries distort the average. A marketing team may use mode to identify the most common customer rating.

## Common Student Doubts

Q1. When should median be used instead of mean?  
A1. Median should be used when the dataset has extreme values or outliers.

Q2. Can there be more than one mode?  
A2. Yes. If two values appear with the same highest frequency, the data can have two modes.

Q3. Is mean always the best average?  
A3. No. Mean is affected by extreme values. Median may be better when data is skewed.
