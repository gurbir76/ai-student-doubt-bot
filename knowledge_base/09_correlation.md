# Correlation

## Topic Area
Business Statistics - Correlation and Regression

## Learning Level
Beginner to Intermediate

## Concept Explanation
Correlation measures the strength and direction of the relationship between two variables.

It tells whether two variables move together, move in opposite directions, or have little linear relationship.

Correlation is usually represented by the correlation coefficient r.

## Key Formula or Value Range
The correlation coefficient r lies between -1 and +1.

## Interpretation
- r = +1 means perfect positive correlation.
- r = -1 means perfect negative correlation.
- r = 0 means no linear correlation.
- Positive correlation means both variables tend to increase together.
- Negative correlation means one variable increases while the other decreases.

## Simple Example
If advertising spend increases and sales also increase, there may be positive correlation.

If product price increases and demand decreases, there may be negative correlation.

## Business Use Case
Businesses use correlation to study relationships such as:

- Advertising spend and sales
- Price and demand
- Training hours and productivity
- Customer satisfaction and repeat purchases
- Delivery time and customer complaints
- Employee engagement and retention

## Common Student Doubts

Q1. Does correlation mean causation?  
A1. No. Correlation shows a relationship, but it does not prove that one variable causes the other.

Q2. What does r = 0.8 mean?  
A2. It means there is a strong positive linear relationship.

Q3. What does r = -0.7 mean?  
A3. It means there is a strong negative linear relationship.

Q4. What does r = 0 mean?  
A4. It means there is no linear relationship between the two variables.

Q5. Can correlation be greater than 1?  
A5. No. Correlation always lies between -1 and +1.
