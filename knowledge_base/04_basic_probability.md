# Basic Probability

## Topic Area
Business Statistics - Probability Basics

## Learning Level
Beginner to Intermediate

## Concept Explanation
Probability measures the chance of an event happening. It is always between 0 and 1.

A probability of 0 means the event will not happen. A probability of 1 means the event will definitely happen.

## Key Formula
Probability = Number of favorable outcomes / Total number of possible outcomes

## Formula Meaning
- Favorable outcomes are the outcomes we are interested in.
- Total possible outcomes are all possible results of the experiment.

## Simple Example
If a coin is tossed, there are two possible outcomes:

Head or Tail

Probability of getting Head:

P(Head) = 1 / 2 = 0.5

So, there is a 50% chance of getting Head.

## Business Use Case
A business may use probability to estimate the chance of customer purchase, product defect, loan default, project delay, or market demand.

## Common Student Doubts

Q1. Can probability be greater than 1?  
A1. No. Probability always lies between 0 and 1.

Q2. Can probability be negative?  
A2. No. Probability cannot be negative.

Q3. What does probability 0.5 mean?  
A3. It means there is a 50% chance of the event happening.

Q4. What is an event?  
A4. An event is an outcome or group of outcomes whose probability we want to calculate.
