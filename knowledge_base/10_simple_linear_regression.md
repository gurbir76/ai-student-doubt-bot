# Simple Linear Regression

## Topic Area
Business Statistics - Correlation and Regression

## Learning Level
Beginner to Intermediate

## Concept Explanation
Simple linear regression is used to study and predict the relationship between one independent variable and one dependent variable.

It creates a straight-line equation that explains how the dependent variable changes when the independent variable changes.

## Key Formula
Y = a + bX

## Formula Meaning
- Y is the dependent variable.
- X is the independent variable.
- a is the intercept.
- b is the slope.

## Simple Example
Suppose a company wants to predict sales based on advertising spend.

Regression equation:

Sales = a + b × Advertising Spend

If b is positive, sales tend to increase when advertising spend increases.

If b is negative, sales tend to decrease when advertising spend increases.

## Business Use Case
Businesses use simple linear regression to:

- Forecast sales
- Estimate demand
- Predict cost
- Understand price impact
- Analyze productivity drivers
- Study advertising impact
- Estimate delivery time based on order volume

## Common Student Doubts

Q1. What is the dependent variable?  
A1. The dependent variable is the variable we want to predict or explain.

Q2. What is the independent variable?  
A2. The independent variable is the variable used to predict or explain the dependent variable.

Q3. What does slope mean?  
A3. Slope shows how much Y changes when X increases by one unit.

Q4. What does intercept mean?  
A4. Intercept is the predicted value of Y when X is zero.

Q5. Is regression the same as correlation?  
A5. No. Correlation measures the strength and direction of a relationship. Regression creates an equation for prediction or explanation.
