# Hypothesis Testing

## Topic Area
Business Statistics - Hypothesis Testing

## Learning Level
Beginner to Intermediate

## Concept Explanation
Hypothesis testing is a statistical method used to make decisions about a population based on sample data.

It helps check whether there is enough evidence to support or reject a claim.

A hypothesis is an assumption or statement that we want to test using data.

## Key Terms
- Null hypothesis means the default assumption.
- Alternative hypothesis means the claim or change we want to test.
- Significance level is the acceptable risk of rejecting the null hypothesis when it is actually true.
- P-value helps decide whether the result is statistically significant.
- Sample data is used to make a decision about the larger population.

## Simple Process
1. State the null hypothesis.
2. State the alternative hypothesis.
3. Choose the significance level.
4. Collect sample data.
5. Calculate the test statistic or p-value.
6. Compare the result with the decision rule.
7. Make a conclusion in simple language.

## Simple Example
A company claims that its average delivery time is 2 days.

Null hypothesis:
The average delivery time is 2 days.

Alternative hypothesis:
The average delivery time is not 2 days.

The company collects sample delivery data and tests whether the claim appears reasonable.

## Business Use Case
Managers use hypothesis testing to test claims about:

- Average delivery time
- Customer satisfaction
- Product quality
- Sales improvement
- Marketing campaign impact
- Process improvement
- Employee productivity

## Common Student Doubts

Q1. What is the null hypothesis?  
A1. The null hypothesis is the default assumption. It usually says there is no change, no effect, or no difference.

Q2. What is the alternative hypothesis?  
A2. The alternative hypothesis is the claim we are trying to find evidence for. It usually says there is a change, effect, or difference.

Q3. Does hypothesis testing prove something with 100% certainty?  
A3. No. It helps make decisions based on sample evidence and probability.

Q4. Why do we need hypothesis testing in business?  
A4. Businesses use it to make data-based decisions instead of relying only on opinion.
