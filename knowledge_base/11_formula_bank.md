## Measures of Central Tendency

### Mean
Mean = Sum of observations / Number of observations

### Median
Median is the middle value after arranging data in ascending order.

### Mode
Mode is the most frequently occurring value.

---

## Measures of Dispersion

### Range
Range = Maximum value - Minimum value

### Population Variance
σ² = Σ(x - μ)² / N

### Population Standard Deviation
σ = √[Σ(x - μ)² / N]

### Sample Variance
s² = Σ(x - x̄)² / (n - 1)

### Sample Standard Deviation
s = √[Σ(x - x̄)² / (n - 1)]

### Formula Meaning
- x means each individual value.
- μ means population mean.
- x̄ means sample mean.
- N means population size.
- n means sample size.
- σ² means population variance.
- σ means population standard deviation.
- s² means sample variance.
- s means sample standard deviation.

## Probability

### Basic Probability
P(A) = Number of favorable outcomes / Total number of possible outcomes

### Conditional Probability
P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)

### Complement Rule
P(A') = 1 - P(A)

### Addition Rule for Mutually Exclusive Events
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)

---

## Normal Distribution

### Normal Distribution Notation
X ~ N(μ, σ²)

### Z-Score
Z = (X - μ) / σ

### Formula Meaning
- X means the observed value.
- μ means mean.
- σ means standard deviation.
- Z means standard score.

---

## Hypothesis Testing

### P-Value Decision Rule
If p-value ≤ α, reject H0.

If p-value > α, do not reject H0.

### Formula Meaning
- p-value means probability value.
- α means significance level.
- H0 means null hypothesis.

---

## Correlation

### Correlation Coefficient
r lies between -1 and +1.

### Interpretation
- r close to +1 means strong positive correlation.
- r close to -1 means strong negative correlation.
- r close to 0 means weak or no linear correlation.

---

## Simple Linear Regression

### Regression Equation
Y = a + bX

### Formula Meaning
- Y is the dependent variable.
- X is the independent variable.
- a is the intercept.
- b is the slope.
