# Introduction to Statistics

## Topic Area
Business Statistics - Introduction to Statistics

## Learning Level
Beginner to Intermediate

## Concept Explanation
Statistics is the process of collecting, organizing, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting data.

In business, statistics helps managers make better decisions using facts and evidence instead of only guesswork or opinion.

Statistics is useful when a business wants to understand customers, sales, costs, quality, employee performance, risks, or market trends.

## Key Ideas
- Data means facts, numbers, or observations collected for analysis.
- Statistics helps convert raw data into useful information.
- Descriptive statistics summarizes data.
- Inferential statistics uses sample data to make conclusions about a larger population.

## Types of Data
Data can be classified in different ways:

### Qualitative Data
Qualitative data describes categories or labels.

Examples:
- Gender
- Product category
- Customer feedback type
- Payment method

### Quantitative Data
Quantitative data is numerical.

Examples:
- Sales amount
- Age
- Salary
- Number of customers
- Delivery time

## Simple Example
A shop records daily sales for one week:

Monday: ₹10,000  
Tuesday: ₹12,000  
Wednesday: ₹9,000  
Thursday: ₹15,000  
Friday: ₹14,000  

Using statistics, the shop owner can calculate average sales, identify high-performing days, and plan inventory better.

## Business Use Case
A company may use statistics to:
- Forecast sales
- Understand customer satisfaction
- Compare branch performance
- Monitor product quality
- Analyze employee productivity
- Measure business risk
- Support management decisions

## Common Student Doubts

Q1. Why do business students study statistics?  
A1. Business students study statistics because managers need to make decisions using data. Statistics helps them analyze sales, customers, costs, quality, and performance.

Q2. What is the difference between data and information?  
A2. Data is raw facts. Information is processed data that is useful for decision-making.

Q3. What is descriptive statistics?  
A3. Descriptive statistics summarizes data using tools like mean, median, mode, charts, tables, variance, and standard deviation.

Q4. What is inferential statistics?  
A4. Inferential statistics uses sample data to make conclusions about a larger population.
